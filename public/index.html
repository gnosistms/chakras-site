    Anatomy layers

    The positions of the chakras and their stems in this graphic were determined by synthesizing descriptions from the books and lectures of Samael Aun Weor, along with the sources that he cited as references. In some cases, the precise locations were not completely clear. We have written a document explaining how we determined the locations in those difficult cases and citing all of our references.
